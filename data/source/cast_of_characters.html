Alaska Mammoth

Cast of Characters:

A.A. Anderson

An “old intimate friend” of HFO, usurps LSQ’s role by offering to go examine claim for free, then finds it impossible to get to Nome, allowing LSQ back off the bench.

H.C. Bumpus

Director AMNH

Chilberg

President of 1909 Alaska-Yukon-Pacific Exposition

E.W. Graham

Finder of purported mammoth, partner of J.H. Startsman

Madison Grant

Secretary, NY Zoological Society. Goes to Alaska with LSQ 1907, assists up to Nome. Also President of the Taylor Creek Ditch Company, whose VP/Gen Manager is Andrew J. Stone

Morris K. Jesup

Philanthropist and president of AMNH.

F.A. Lucas

Director AMNH after Bumpus.

W.D. Matthew

Curator (?) in dept. of VP, AMNH. Reports on Startsman letters, recommends LSQ for job.

Edmond S. Meany

University of Washington professor, go-between for A-Y-P people and AMNH

J.P. Morgan Jr.

Paid for half of both 1907 and 1908 expeditions.

Henry Fairfield Osborn

Curator of VP, AMNH.

Percy Pyne

Paid for half of both 1907 and 1908 expeditions.
